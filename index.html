02 / About me

About Me.

From classroom experience to questions about language and learning.

LANGUAGE·LEARNING·EDUCATION

I am currently pursuing postgraduate studies in Language Studies at City University of Hong Kong. I previously studied Chinese Language and Literature (Teacher Education) at Zhejiang International Studies University.

My interests developed through teacher education and classroom experience. During my undergraduate studies, I completed teaching internships in different educational settings. These experiences encouraged me to explore how languages are taught and learned.

I have also gained research experience in TESOL and language education. My previous work includes a project on social media and English language learning and a published conference paper on the professional challenges faced by Chinese female TESOL teachers.

I am particularly interested in connecting language education with applied linguistics. In the future, I hope to explore how linguistic research and AI-assisted technologies can support teaching and learning.
